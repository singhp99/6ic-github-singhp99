##Importance of Thermostat Coding

With the increasing energy bills not only due to inflation but energy becoming more expensive resource, it is more important to consider the settings of our thermostats to lower our bills. The tolerance (mentioned in the [how-to page](how_to_thermo.md)) if higher, can provide an approach to lower energy bills. 

It is also believed that, when away from home, if the HVAC is turned up by a few degrees it is possible to lower energy cost, specifically in the summer. Now, a simple solution could be to manually increase temperature each day and lower temperature each night. However, a much more efficient solution would be to program this action for specific times, say raising the temperature at 10 AM by 8 degrees Fahrenheit and lowering back the temperature at 8 PM, or one could also set the times to those of the beginning of peak hour and the end.
